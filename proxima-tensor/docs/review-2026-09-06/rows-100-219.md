## Evidence-bound synthesis for the stated question

Across these rows, the records supporting “llama.cpp beats Proxima” are not a
single scalar: (1) same-window Metal/OpenChat rows show Proxima’s short-run
prefill and total wall-clock trailing llama; (2) later per-shape GPU rows point
to tile/barrier, Q4_K unpack, output-head byte/shape, and dispatch/path
differences; (3) CPU/BGE rows repeatedly find route eligibility, lowering,
arena/cache regime, and small-operation dispatch overhead before the kernel is
actually reached. The log also contains explicit corrections and retractions:
ROW 120 removes the mislabeled 4.2x kernel advantage, ROW 117/118 removes the
missing-work-stealing premise, and ROW 218 removes the flattering torch cell.

The remaining evidence is workload- and phase-bound. Rows that report a BGE,
MNIST, format, or training result do not establish a Q4_K llama decode cause;
rows with derived values, missing paired arms, host CoV above the stated floor,
or unmeasured residuals cannot be promoted to current baseline claims.
