# fp6-qat-ablation -- slices

Each slice: one commit, one behaviour change, one validation command, one expected count. Update the checkbox and the note IN THE SAME COMMIT as the slice. Slice N discharges ACN of SPEC.md (same number, same command, same count). Slices 1, 2, 5, 6, 10, 11, 14 and 19 are marked `behind gpu.lock` and run serially on the box's GPU, never overlapping another GPU gate, agent or timing run, with the box load checked first; slices 2, 10 and 11 are also TIMING cells on the quiet box. All other slices are CPU-only. Model-loading runs one at a time. `GRANITE`, `OUT`, `IDS`, `CONFIG`, `CORPUS`, `QAT_BASE`, `FEAT`, `COMMON` are bound by the assignment block in the SPEC's acceptance criteria section; the SPEC's `### command blocks` section holds the commands in the form to run. Commands run from the repo root. Dependencies on the fp6-e3m2-codec spec: slice 13 (`convert_codec`, landed) for slice 3; slices 14 (`convert_codec` example) and 16 (`codec_token_agreement` example) for slice 14 here; both are open in `proxima-tensor/specs/fp6-e3m2-codec/TASKS.md` and land there, not here. Dependencies inside this spec: 2 needs 1; 3 has none; 4 needs 1 and 3; 5 needs 3; 6 has none; 7 has none; 8 needs 7; 9 needs 4 and 7; 10 needs 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9 and 19; 11 needs 5 and 10; 12 needs 10 and 11; 13 needs 10; 14 needs 11 and 13; 15 needs 10 and 11; 16 needs 10 and 11; 17 needs 12, 14, 15 and 16; 18 needs 17; 19 needs 4 (its forward-only graph and checkpoint load) and lands before slice 10 so the loss lines of 10 and 11 are produced by a checked function. If slice 1 prints a nonzero `metal_reject`, the rejected ops land as slices numbered 1a, 1b, ... between 1 and 2 (each with its own test count), and the order is recorded under `## struck` as an amendment, not by renumbering.
