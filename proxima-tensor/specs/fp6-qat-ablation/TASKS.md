# fp6-qat-ablation -- slices

Each slice: one commit, one behaviour change, one validation command, one expected count. Update the checkbox and the note IN THE SAME COMMIT as the slice. Slice N discharges ACN of SPEC.md (same number, same command, same count). Slices 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 11, 12, 15 and 20 are marked `behind gpu.lock` and run serially on the box's GPU, never overlapping another GPU gate, agent or timing run, with the box load checked first; slices 3, 11 and 12 are also TIMING cells on the quiet box. All other slices are CPU-only. Model-loading runs one at a time. `GRANITE`, `OUT`, `IDS`, `CONFIG`, `CORPUS`, `QAT_BASE`, `FEAT`, `COMMON` are bound by the assignment block in the SPEC's acceptance criteria section; the SPEC's `### command blocks` section holds the commands in the form to run. Commands run from the repo root. Dependencies on the fp6-e3m2-codec spec: slice 13 (`convert_codec`, landed) for slice 4; slices 14 (`convert_codec` example) and 16 (`codec_token_agreement` example) for slice 15 here; both are open in `proxima-tensor/specs/fp6-e3m2-codec/TASKS.md` and land there, not here. Dependency on the float-layout-dtype spec: slice 2 here is the gate on its slice 19 (`proxima-tensor/specs/float-layout-dtype/TASKS.md`, AC19, 234), which lands there, not here; nothing in this spec adds a bf16 path. Dependencies inside this spec: 1a needs 1; 1b needs 1; 2 has none beyond that spec; 3 needs 1, 1a, 1b and 2; 4 has none; 5 needs 1 and 4; 6 needs 2 and 4; 7 needs 2; 8 has none; 9 needs 8; 10 needs 5 and 8; 11 needs 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10 and 20; 12 needs 6 and 11; 13 needs 11 and 12; 14 needs 11; 15 needs 12 and 14; 16 needs 11 and 12; 17 needs 11 and 12; 18 needs 13, 15, 16 and 17; 19 needs 18; 20 needs 5 (its forward-only graph and checkpoint load) and lands before slice 11 so the loss lines of 11 and 12 are produced by a checked function. If slice 1 prints a nonzero `metal_reject`, the rejected ops land as slices numbered 1a, 1b, ... between 1 and 3 (each with its own test count), and the order is recorded under `## struck` as an amendment, not by renumbering.
