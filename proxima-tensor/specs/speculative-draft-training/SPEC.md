# speculative-draft-training

status: draft
owner: brian bruggeman
created: 2026-10-12

## problem

The three draft methods `speculative-draft-eagle3`, `speculative-draft-dflash` and `speculative-draft-dspark` need a draft model for gemma4 E2B (and granite 1b if its shape allows), and none exists: llama.cpp's published drafts target other models, and the owner's direction (2026-10-12) is to train our own drafts for the E2B rather than match llama.cpp's draft zoo ("I don't care that llama has nothing for it"). Without a trained draft those specs can only run a seeded random draft, which proves losslessness and plumbing but cannot show that the target's hidden-state taps carry signal, so their `accepted` > 0 gate (RL2) is blocked. proxima has the pieces of a training path but not the path: reverse-mode autodiff over a `proxima_tensor` program with a census example that differentiates granite's forward (`proxima-autograd/src/adjoint.rs:214-216`, `proxima-model-interop/examples/qat/graph.rs:38-57`), cross-entropy, AdamW, a host-buffer loop, safetensors persistence and Metal execution of an autograd-produced backward graph (`omega/tests/training_step_parity.rs`) -- all proven on toy MLPs or on the granite census, none on a draft-shaped graph. No data pipeline turns the target's own generations and hidden-state taps into training records, no draft vocabulary reduction exists, no trained state is exported to the GGUF the draft descriptors read, and granite's profile does not arm a verify program (`grep -c '^speculative_verify = true' proxima-model-interop/src/profiles/granitemoe.toml` prints 0; gemma4's prints 1). This spec adds that path with Rust examples only (no Python, no scripts), trains EAGLE3, DFlash and DSpark drafts for gemma4 E2B and, when the granite verify program exists, for granite 1b, by self-distillation: the labels are the target's own greedy generations on a stated corpus.

## refutation condition

Written before evidence: if, after 1024 training steps on gemma4 E2B for any one method, the held-out cross-entropy is not below the random-initialization baseline (AC10 prints `"heldout_decreased":false`), or the trained draft's `mean_accepted_per_step` on the 51-prompt evaluation corpus (the `speculative_lossless_corpus` summary line) is below 0.5 while the seeded random draft's is at or below 0.05 (AC16, AC17), then the data-and-graph design (R2, R3, R5) does not teach the draft the target's behaviour at this scale and must be redesigned -- more steps or a larger corpus are not a repair of a loss that does not decrease. If the random draft's `mean_accepted_per_step` is above 0.05, the measurement is not measuring training and the AC16 relation is void.

## requirements

| id | requirement | testable in isolation |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | the training corpus is stated and disjoint from evaluation: 1024 prose records (paragraphs of `proxima-model-interop/examples/data/war_and_peace.txt`, 3,274,130 bytes) and 1024 code records (chunks of `proxima-model-interop/src/**/*.rs`) for training, 64 plus 64 held-out records, none sharing text with each other and none containing the first 200 bytes of any of the 51 prompts of `proxima-model-interop/examples/data/speculative_corpus.jsonl` (17 chat, 17 code, 17 rag); the overlap detector is itself validated by a planted prompt | yes |
| R2 | self-distillation records: for each corpus record the target's own plain greedy decode generates at least 16 and at most 64 tokens, and the example writes `ids` (prompt ids and generated ids, JSONL) and the target's hidden-state taps at the draft descriptor's layers for every prompt and generated position (safetensors, through the readout list of `decode-as-data` R5), so every label is the target's own next token and every feature is the target's own activation | yes |
| R3 | each method's training step is one `proxima_tensor` program: the draft's `build_forward` graph (from the method spec's descriptor), softmax cross-entropy against the target's next-token labels (`proxima-autograd/src/loss.rs:153-163`), differentiated with respect to the draft's own weights only (`proxima-autograd/src/adjoint.rs:266-272`), with an AdamW update appended (`proxima-autograd/src/optimizer.rs:694-855`); the target's weights and taps enter as `Op::Input` leaves and are never differentiated | yes |
| R4 | the draft vocabulary is reduced to the 32768 most frequent generated tokens of the training records with a `d2t` map (draft id to target id), so the logits row is `[seq, 32768]` and not `[seq, 262144]`; labels outside the reduced vocabulary are masked and their fraction is reported as `coverage` | yes |
| R5 | every draft graph differentiates with zero refusals: a census over the EAGLE3, DFlash and DSpark training programs prints one `refusals=0` line per method, and a program containing a known-refused op prints a nonzero refusal count (the control), following `proxima-model-interop/examples/qat_census.rs:48-60` | yes |
| R6 | the draft must be able to learn: with 64 steps on a single 256-token sequence the training loss falls from within 0.5 of `ln(32768)` (10.397) to below 0.1 on every method (the overfit control), on the CPU path (`proxima-autograd/src/train.rs:171-217`) | yes |
| R7 | the CPU and Metal step agree: the same one training step, executed on CPU (`proxima-autograd/src/train.rs:69-95`) and on Metal (`omega/src/backend.rs:1157-1162`), gives losses within 1e-3 (pre-registered, status plausible, not yet measured), with a perturbed control that is rejected | yes |
| R8 | the training run is the product: 1024 steps of one 256-token sequence each, AdamW, seeded initialization, a train-loss line per step and a held-out evaluation line at step 0 and every 128 steps (9 lines), written as JSONL with one `kind` field per line (`train` or `heldout`) and one numeric `loss` field; state is saved with `persist::save_state` (`proxima-autograd/src/persist.rs:117-144`) and resumed with `persist::load_state` (`:170-235`) | yes |
| R9 | the loss decreases against a stated baseline: the random-initialization held-out cross-entropy at step 0 is within 0.5 of `ln(32768)`, and the final held-out cross-entropy is below the step-0 value and at most 6.0 nats (pre-registered, status plausible, not derived from any measurement in the tree), with every logged loss a finite number | yes |
| R10 | granite 1b: `granitemoe.toml` arms `speculative_verify = true` and its verify program keeps one logits row per new position, or the typed `speculative_verify` refusal is recorded as the outcome and no granite draft is trained; when armed, granite's draft is trained by the same path with the granite hidden width and tokenizer | yes |
| R11 | the trained draft beats the untrained draft on held-out prompts: on the 51-prompt evaluation corpus the trained draft's `mean_accepted_per_step` is at least 0.5 and the seeded random draft's is at most 0.05, both read from the `summary` line of `speculative_lossless_corpus` | yes |
| R12 | the trained draft is lossless: the method specs' ACL2 (and ACL4 for granite) print `51` identical prompts, each with `n_drafted` > 0 and `accepted` > 0; this spec adds no second copy of that gate | yes |
| R13 | export and round trip: the trained masters are written as a GGUF with `proxima-gguf`'s writer (`proxima-gguf/src/writer.rs:65-104`) carrying the keys and tensor names of the method spec's descriptor (R1/R2 there), the serving-path draft forward on the exported file equals the training-graph forward on the same weights within 1e-4, and an export with one tensor perturbed is rejected by the same comparator | yes |
| R14 | determinism: two runs with the same seed, data order and initial weights agree on every held-out loss within 0.001 | yes |
| R15 | no Python and no scripts: every harness is a Rust example under `proxima-model-interop/examples/` behind a new feature `draft-train` shaped like the existing `qat` feature (`proxima-model-interop/Cargo.toml:327`); no new type is added to a library crate unless a requirement above forces one | yes |
| R16 | cost is recorded, not gated: wall time per run and peak RSS of each training run from `/usr/bin/time -l`, with the example's own clock agreeing within 2% | yes |

## architecture

Existing training primitives, each read at the cited line this session:

| primitive | where | what it gives |
|---|---|---|
| adjoint transform | `proxima-autograd/src/adjoint.rs:214-216` (`differentiate`), `:266-272` (`differentiate_wanted`), `:137-142` (`Differentiated`) | reverse-mode over a `&[Op]` program for a scalar loss node; `differentiate_wanted` restricts the transform to chosen weight nodes, which is how the target stays frozen |
| gathered gradients | `proxima-autograd/src/adjoint.rs:137-142` (the `gathered` field), `omega/tests/training_step_parity.rs:6-15` | an embedding-table gather's gradient is a host-side scatter-add outside `Differentiated.program`; the draft's token embedding is one, so it is applied on the host, not in the graph |
| loss | `proxima-autograd/src/loss.rs:153-163` (`softmax_cross_entropy`) | the scalar objective, built as graph nodes |
| optimizer | `proxima-autograd/src/optimizer.rs:70-82` (`AdamConfig`), `:694-855` (`adamw_step`) | the update as elementwise nodes over `Op::Input` leaves for moments and step |
| host loop | `proxima-autograd/src/train.rs:69-95` (`train_step`), `:171-217` (`fit`) | named host buffers bound per step, updated buffers rebound by name; CPU only |
| persistence | `proxima-autograd/src/persist.rs:117-144` (`save_state`), `:170-235` (`load_state`) | `train::State` through safetensors, shapes taken from the program |
| low-precision view | `proxima-autograd/src/low_precision.rs:20-39` (`weight_view`) | fake-quant weight views over f32 masters with the identity straight-through estimator; not needed by this spec's f32 masters, named so it is not re-invented if the draft is later trained quantization-aware |
| Metal execution of an autograd program | `omega/src/backend.rs:1157-1162` (`execute_plan_named`), `omega/tests/training_step_parity.rs` (MLP forward, backward and Adam on Metal against the CPU oracle) | the backward graph is an ordinary program, so Metal runs it with no autograd code of its own |
| differentiating a real model | `proxima-model-interop/examples/qat/graph.rs:38-57` (`step_graph`: `build_forward`, symbol binding, cross-entropy, `differentiate_wanted`), `proxima-model-interop/examples/qat_census.rs:48-60`, `proxima-tensor/specs/fp6-qat-ablation/SPEC.md` R1a-R1c | the census over granite's forward at 1 and 24 layers, and the affine operand-map adjoint it required |
| GGUF writer | `proxima-gguf/src/writer.rs:65-104` (`write_complete`) | the export target |

What is missing becomes a requirement above: draft-shaped training graphs (R3), the data pipeline and tap capture (R1, R2), draft vocabulary reduction (R4), the draft census (R5), Metal-driven rebinding of a draft training step (R7, R8), export to the draft descriptors' GGUF (R13) and granite's verify program (R10). The tap capture depends on the `decode-as-data` readout list (R5 there, slice 3), which is why slice 0 names it.

- **self-distillation (R1/R2)**: the labels are the target's own greedy next tokens, so the draft learns to predict exactly what greedy acceptance will check; no external teacher, no third-party text beyond the prose and code the corpus names.
- **one graph per method (R3)**: the EAGLE3 program is encoder plus one decoder layer over the three taps; the DFlash program is the masked-block draft over the taps (DFlash1, no conv or selector); the DSpark program is the DFlash program plus the Markov head and the optional confidence head (`speculative-draft-dspark` R4, R5). Each is the method spec's `build_forward` graph, not a second definition.
- **evaluation through serving (R11/R12)**: the acceptance and losslessness numbers come from the serving path (`speculative_lossless_corpus`), not from a training-side metric, so a train/serve mismatch cannot hide (R13 checks the same thing at the tensor level).

### decisions

| decision | chosen | why not the alternative |
|---|---|---|
| training data | the target's own generations on prose and code from this repository | an external instruction dataset adds a corpus we would have to vendor and license; self-distillation matches the greedy acceptance check exactly |
| draft vocabulary | 32768 most frequent generated tokens with `d2t` | a full 262144-row logits per position makes the loss program's activations and their adjoints the memory bottleneck on this machine; the `d2t` tensor is already a field of the EAGLE3 descriptor (`speculative-draft-eagle3` R1) |
| masters | f32 | the quantization-aware path is its own spec (`fp6-qat-ablation`); this spec trains an f32 draft and exports f32 |
| evaluation corpus | the 51 prompts of `speculative_corpus.jsonl`, never trained on | R1's overlap count enforces it |
| sampling temperature for labels | greedy | the acceptance check in the method specs is greedy; sampled labels would train toward a different target |

## out of scope

- matching llama.cpp's draft models, their formats beyond the key and tensor names the method specs read, and any Qwen, gemma 26B/31B or other large model: the owner restricts test models to gemma4 E2B and granite 1b (2026-10-12)
- MTP: the published assistant draft is used as is (`speculative-draft-mtp`); no MTP head is trained
- DFlash2 (convolution and selector) and non-vanilla Markov heads: DFlash1 and the vanilla DSpark head are trained; a DFlash2 variant is a follow-on within this spec's path once DFlash1 clears AC16
- quantization-aware training and bf16 masters: `fp6-qat-ablation`; this spec's masters and export are f32
- distributed or multi-GPU training: this machine is the only machine
- tuning for acceptance beyond the pre-registered gates: AC16 states the floor, not an optimum
- the readout list itself (`decode-as-data` R5, slice 3) and the draft descriptors (the method specs): consumed here

## risks

| risk | likelihood | what it costs | what we do about it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1024 steps of one 256-token sequence under-trains the draft, so AC10 passes and AC16 fails | medium | the trained draft accepts less than 0.5 per step | the refutation condition names it; the fix is a measured change to the data size or steps recorded against this spec, not a threshold edit |
| the target's hidden-state taps for gemma4 need the Padded engine's residual nodes, which do not exist yet | certain today | R2 cannot capture features | slice 0 depends on `decode-as-data` slice 3, whose readout list is the prerequisite in every method spec |
| the draft training program does not fit on this Mac's GPU alongside the frozen target | medium | the run needs a memory design | R4's reduced vocabulary is the first lever; R16 records peak footprint; the target's taps are precomputed (R2) so the target is not resident during training |
| prose and Rust code do not cover the chat and rag categories of the evaluation corpus | medium | trained acceptance is lower on those categories | AC16 reads the whole corpus; the per-category counts are in the per-prompt lines for inspection and are not gated |
