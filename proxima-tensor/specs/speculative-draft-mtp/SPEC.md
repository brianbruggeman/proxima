## problem

proxima-model-interop has zero support for llama.cpp's `draft-mtp` speculation type (`common_speculative_impl_draft_mtp`, `common/speculative.cpp:1331-1747`) -- no NextN tensor names in its GGUF binds, no hidden-state readout analogous to `llama_get_embeddings_nextn` (`src/llama-ext.h:105`), and no descriptor for the assistant sidecar family (the GGUF whose `general.architecture` value is the `LLM_ARCH_GEMMA4_ASSISTANT` name, `gemma4-assistant`, `src/llama-arch.cpp:60`). The gemma4 E2B GGUF that proxima loads carries no MTP head (0 occurrences of `nextn.pre_projection` and of `nextn.eh_proj` in its tensor directory, checked 2026-09-28), so the pair under test is gemma4 E2B plus its published assistant draft model, a second GGUF. The owner's direction (2026-10-12) drops the Qwen MTP fixture and every Qwen-only requirement. This sub-spec makes `draft-mtp` drafting available as ServingConfig data -- a second `ModelDescriptor` driving its own `ServingState` (`decode-as-data` R6) that reads the target's embedding table and shared KV -- and gates it on one correctness relation: speculative decoding with greedy acceptance is lossless, so its output must equal the target's own plain greedy decode token for token (incumbent ~ plain decode is an owner-stated premise this spec does not re-measure, then speculative == plain decode). llama.cpp `f1ea20621`'s own `--spec-type draft-mtp` output on the same gemma pair stays as a secondary check, because that pair exists in llama.cpp. It adds no technique-named type (`decode-as-data` AC9, R11).

## refutation condition

Written before evidence: if, after slice 1 obtains the real sidecar GGUF, the sidecar graph (`src/models/gemma4-assistant.cpp:84-200`) cannot be reproduced losslessly from `ModelDescriptor` fields, a readout list and `Op::Input` leaves -- i.e. it needs a graph concept (a new `Op`, or a family-keyed branch in the loop) that the descriptor cannot carry as data, observed as fewer than 51 prompts identical to plain greedy on the 51-prompt corpus with a correct verify path -- this sub-spec's architecture section is wrong and must be redone against the actual graph shape, not patched.

## architecture

Nothing here is a new type. MTP is a second descriptor with its own `ServingState`, driven through the four pipes of `decode-as-data` (propose, shape, accept, commit). Its row in that spec's table is "extra readout roots on the target program (nextn block) | chain | sample-and-match | prefix", specialised here to the sidecar, whose readout roots are the sidecar's own `t_h_nextn`.

- **detection (R1)** is a free function over `&ParsedGguf`, not a method on a type: detection needs no bound weights. It lives beside the existing tensor-name lookup (`proxima-model-interop/src/bind.rs:298-304` `find_tensor`, `proxima-gguf/src/parser.rs`); slice 4 decides which crate by reading their neighbors first.
- **hidden-state readout (R3/R4)**: the target's final hidden row per verified position is a readout at the final layer (`decode-as-data` R5), the analogue of `llama_get_embeddings_nextn_ith` (`src/llama-ext.h:108`). `BoundProgram::hidden_root` (`proxima-model-interop/src/lowering.rs:137`) is the final-norm activation of the last row only; the all-positions tap is the readout list, `decode-as-data` slice 3, a prerequisite named in TASKS slice 0.
- **sidecar descriptor (R2/R3)** is a second `ModelDescriptor` (`proxima-tensor/src/spec/descriptor.rs:130-296`) loaded by the same path as the existing draft-model support (`proxima-model-interop/src/generate/draft_model.rs:174-183`, `load_draft`) and driven by its own `ServingState` instance. What is new is only that its embedding table and its attention KV are the target's: descriptor fields naming the source, resolved at construction into a borrowed read-only handle to the target state. The shape (field names, how the draft `ServingState` aliases the target's cache layers) is the design slice's output (TASKS slice 3), a signature there, not a settled design here.
- **draft loop (R4/R5)** is the propose pipe configured by the `draft-mtp` config value; accept is sample-and-match; commit is prefix truncate. The sidecar's `is_mem_shared` behaviour is a branch on the descriptor's KV-source field in the propose pipe, not a second pipe, because the accept and rollback shape is identical.
- **lossless harness (RL1-RL5)**: the `speculative_lossless_corpus` example is created by `speculative-draft-eagle3` slice 1 and consumed here with `--drafter draft-mtp`.

### decisions

| decision | chosen | why not the alternative |
|---|---|---|
| pair under test | gemma4 E2B plus `google/gemma-4-E2B-it-assistant` (converted) or a third-party GGUF of it | the owner restricts test models to gemma4 E2B and granite 1b and asks for the published assistant draft on gemma4 E2B only; no granite assistant exists |
| single-GGUF MTP form (NextN block inside the target) | out of scope, typed unsupported-form error | its only fixtures were Qwen3.5/3.6 checkpoints, which the owner removed; the profiles that would declare the block (`qwen35.toml`, `qwen35moe.toml`) are not test models |
| correctness oracle | the target's own plain greedy decode over the 51-prompt corpus; llama.cpp ids on the same pair as a secondary check | the published assistant is the draft by definition, so there is no draft to train for it, and losslessness is the property speculation must hold |
| MTP as a second descriptor | sidecar as a second descriptor | `decode-as-data` R5/R6/R11 supersede a per-family capability method; a name check (`general.architecture` equality in code) is the forbidden shape, a key read feeding a profile is the allowed one |
| sidecar sharing is descriptor data | embedding source and KV source fields on the draft descriptor | a bespoke program type holding a target handle would be a per-family type (`decode-as-data` R6); the sharing is a property of the descriptor, resolved when the `ServingState` is built (principle 15: a hidden prerequisite found mid-spec is part of the same work, not a footnote) |

## out of scope

- the single-GGUF MTP form (NextN block inside the target: qwen35, qwen35moe, step35, deepseek and the like) and the `mtp_head_present` detector, the NextN readout lowering, `h_prev` plumbing, `pending_h` carryover and catch-up decode that serve it: every fixture for them was a Qwen checkpoint, which the owner removed (2026-10-12); R1 returns a typed unsupported-form error for it
- `chain_heads` mode (`n_mtp_layers > 1` on the non-shared-memory path, `common/speculative.cpp:1421`): never triggers for the shared-memory sidecar; the sidecar's multi-layer nextn block (R3) is one graph pass over `n_layer_nextn` layers under shared memory and is in scope
- MTP sidecar download/auto-resolution (`opts.download_mtp`, the `--mtp` planner flag in `common/arg.cpp:361-417`): proxima's fixtures are local paths; slice 1 names the repos to fetch by hand
- other sidecar sizes (E4B, 26B-A4B, 31B): outside the owner's test models
- training a granite assistant or a trained gemma assistant: the published assistant is the draft here; `speculative-draft-training` covers EAGLE3, DFlash and DSpark
- the readout list itself (`decode-as-data` R5, slice 3) and the descriptor-level family profiles: owned by those specs; this sub-spec consumes them

## risks

| risk | likelihood | what it costs | what we do about it |
|---|---|---|---|
| cross-descriptor weight and KV sharing (R3) has no analogue in the current descriptor/`ServingState` pair | high | the sidecar could need a cache-aliasing capability that ripples into the parent spec's R3a/R3b rollback | named, with a dedicated design slice (TASKS slice 3) before any sidecar implementation slice; AC5 pins alias versus copy |
