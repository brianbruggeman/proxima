# litert-door -- slices

Each slice: one commit, one requirement, one validation command, the AC it repeats. Update the checkbox and the note IN THE SAME COMMIT as the slice, and append the slice's row to `docs/litert-door/discipline.md` once slice 31 has created it (until then the row is held in the note). Slice N discharges the AC named in its row and the requirement that AC names. Slices 25, 26, 28 and 29 load models on Metal or time on the box and run behind `gpu.lock`; slice 27 and the vendor recording runs of slices 12, 15 and 18 time or load the box and run alone: no other agent, build or model process, ollama off, `uptime` load recorded before the run. All other slices are CPU-only and may be written in parallel exports once their dependencies have landed. Each slice lands its test cases in the order its AC lists them, so it stays inside ~30 minutes by adding the cases in separate steps of the same commit.

Export is the headline: slices 1 to 16 deliver the writers, the vendor checks and the disciplined-component core before any reader slice 17 to 19 is needed for the program-to-file direction. The reader slices 7 and 13 come early only because the round trip is the writers' own control.

Slices 6, 12 and 18 add files in the indexer repo as well as vendored files in proxima; each repo gets its own commit and the owner authorizes each.

Slice 1 is the generalization of the ONNX door: `git mv omega/src/onnx_door omega/src/door`, `OnnxDoor` becomes `Door<Read, Write>`, the ONNX reader becomes a pipe in `proxima-onnx`, the 16 landed ONNX door tests keep their names and assertions and change only the construction line. Before it writes any code it runs `git grep -n -E 'pub struct (Door|OnnxDoor)|DoorStage' origin/main -- omega/src` and, if another spec's slice landed a format-generic door (`mlir-interop` slice 1 and `coreml-ane-lane` slice 1 specify the same one), reuses it and shrinks this slice to the missing cases of `door_shared_core_`. Slice 1 also strikes ONNX TASKS slice 5 (`onnx_door_walkthrough`, `10` lines) with the reason that AC3 here supersedes it, and moves the `onnx-door` feature to `onnx-door = ["door", ...]`.
