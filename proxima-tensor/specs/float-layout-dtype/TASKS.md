# float-layout-dtype -- slices

Each slice: one commit, one behaviour change, one validation command, one expected count. Update the checkbox and the note IN THE SAME COMMIT as the slice. Slice N discharges ACN of SPEC.md (same number, same command, same count). Slices 4, 6, 8, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17 and 18 are marked `behind gpu.lock` and run serially on the box's GPU, never overlapping another GPU gate, agent or timing run, with the box load checked first; models load one at a time. `GRANITE`, `E2B`, `IDS`, `OUT`, `LAYOUT_BASE`, `FEAT` are bound by the assignment block in the SPEC's acceptance criteria section; the SPEC's `### command blocks` section holds the commands in the form to run. Commands run from the repo root. Everything new is behind the default-off feature `float-layout-dtype` (omega, proxima-tensor, proxima-gguf, proxima-model-interop passthroughs) until slice 19 flips it default-on and deletes the named variants it replaced.

Dependencies inside this spec: 1 has none; 2 needs 1; 3 needs 1; 4 needs 2 and 3; 5 needs 2 and 4; 6 needs 3, 4 and 5; 7 needs 5; 8 needs 4 and 6; 9 needs 2 and 4; 10 needs 2 and 5; 11 needs 4 and 8; 12 needs 3, 4 and 6; 13 needs 1, 4, 6 and 12; 14 needs 13; 15 needs 14; 16 needs 13, 14 and 15; 17 needs 1, 13, 14 and 15 (the codec shapes it reuses); 18 needs 2, 5 and 13; 19 needs every other slice. Order by the owner's direction: float dtypes (2 to 11), integers with the sign encoding (12), the linear block codecs (13), the K family (14), the codebook family (15), the unnamed layout and the arm census (16), the ten codecs with no decoder checked against vendored ggml output (17), the single evaluate pipe per back end (18), the gates and the flip (19). Every family slice (13, 14, 15) is a bit-exact parity gate against the recorded output of the incumbent decoder it replaces, and ends with the byte-identical llama-ids check on granite and gemma4 E2B; a family slice that exceeds the cap splits by codec as 13a, 13b, ... with the AC kept on the last part and the split recorded under `## struck`.
