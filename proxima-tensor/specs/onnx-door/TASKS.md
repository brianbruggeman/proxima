# onnx-door -- slices

Each slice: one commit, one requirement, one validation command, the AC it repeats. Update the checkbox and the note IN THE SAME COMMIT as the slice. Slice N discharges the AC named in its row and the requirement that AC names. Slices 8, 9, 10, 13, 14, 15 and 16 load models on Metal or time on the box and run behind `gpu.lock`; slices 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 11 and 12 are CPU-only and may be written in parallel exports once their dependencies have landed. Each slice lands its tests in the order its AC lists them, so it stays inside ~30 minutes by adding the cases in separate steps of the same commit. A timed slice (7, 13 to 16) runs alone: no other agent, build or model process on the box, ollama off, `uptime` load recorded before the run.

Slices 6 and 7 add files in the indexer repo as well as the vendored files in proxima; each repo gets its own commit and the owner authorizes each.

The census (slice 1) decides what follows. It was counted from the model bytes while this spec was written: bge-small has 1244 nodes of 19 op types, all among the 48 `lower_node` arms; mnist has 13 nodes of 6 op types, all among them. Slice 2 therefore serves BERT-family exports other than this one, not bge-small, and is the slice the owner may strike.

Dependencies: 1 none; 2 none (after 1 only for the `SUPPORTED_OPS` equality test, which it extends to 52); 3 on 1; 4 on 3; 5 on 3; 6 none; 7 on 6; 8 on 3, 4, 6, 7; 9 on 8; 10 on 3, 4 (its Metal tests on 9's `gpu.lock` turn); 11 on 1; 12 on 3 (the Cargo.toml edit lives in slice 3, so 12 re-runs the check at the tip); 13 on 8, 9; 14 on 13; 15 on 13; 16 on 13.
