# mlir-interop -- slices

Each slice: one commit, one requirement, one validation command, the AC it repeats. Update the checkbox and the note IN THE SAME COMMIT as the slice. Slice N discharges the AC named in its row and the requirement that AC names; a slice whose command is a filtered subset of its AC says so, and the slice that completes the AC runs the full command. Slices 23, 24 and 32 to 34 load models on Metal or time on the box and run behind `gpu.lock`; every other slice is CPU-only and may be written in parallel exports once its dependencies have landed. Each slice lands its tests in the order its AC lists them, so it stays inside ~30 minutes by adding the cases in separate steps of the same commit. A timed slice (29, 32 to 34) runs alone: no other agent, build or model process on the box, ollama off, `uptime` load recorded before the run. No slice runs `mlir-opt` from `PATH`: the tools are at `/opt/homebrew/opt/llvm/bin` (slice 2 pins them).

The measurements decide what follows. Slice 12 (the export census) is the first number: how many ops of the two real decode programs are `faithful`, `repack` and `none`; the expected values in `SPEC.md` are stated so the measurement can contradict them, and a `none` count above 0 is refutation 2, reported before any later export slice proceeds. Slice 8 (the import census) is the second: `mapped`, `host_folded` and `unmapped` for the bge and MNIST dumps; an `unmapped` count above 0 after slice 17 is refutation 3.

Dependencies: 1 none (first checks whether `origin/main` already carries the generalization, and reuses it); 2 none; 3 none; 4 on 3; 5 on 4; 6 on 5, 2; 7 on 5; 8 on 6; 9 on 2, 3; 10 on 9, 2; 11 on 10, 7; 12 on 9, `LoadedModel` accessors (inside the slice); 13 on 10, 12; 14 on 6, 8 and on `onnx-door` slice 3 (landed: the ONNX lowering and census it binds to); 15 on 8; 16 on 15; 17 on 14, 16; 18 on 15, 6; 19 on 3 (config surface) and 10 (memref form); 20 on 1, 11, 13, 14, 17, 18; 21 on 20; 22 on 20 and `onnx-door` slices 7 and 8 (the tract record and `tests/support/bge.rs`); 23 on 22 and `onnx-door` slice 9; 24 on 20; 25 on 3, 10; 26 on 1, 3; 27 on 1, 3; 28 on 6, 8; 29 on 6, 10, 2; 30 on 29 and every other component slice; 31 at the tip; 32 on 23, 24, 29 and `onnx-door` slices 7 and 13; 33 on 32; 34 on 32.
